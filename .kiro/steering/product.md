# Product Overview

**Aplikasi Hub Karyawan** is an internal employee management hub for Sankyu Indonesia (a mining/industrial services company). It serves two user groups:

1. **Admin Portal** — HR/admin staff manage employee master data, leave requests, discipline records, certifications, training, and notifications.
2. **Employee Portal (PWA)** — Employees view their own data, submit leave requests, and receive push notifications on mobile devices.

## Core Domains

- **Employee Master Data** — Personal info, department, job role/level, grade, employment type, group shifts.
- **Leave Management** — Multi-stage approval workflow (foreman → section chief → dept manager), balance tracking, email notifications, revision history.
- **Discipline Records** — Guidance/direction meetings, warning letters, reprimands, suspension letters (linked to PKB articles).
- **License & Certifications** — Employee document tracking with expiry dates; unit-level certifications tied to vendors.
- **Training** — Internal/external training records with participant tracking.
- **Notifications** — Admin notification center with read-state tracking; employee push notifications via Web Push.
- **Group Shifts** — Shift group assignments with foreman mappings.

## Business Context

- The application language is **Indonesian (Bahasa Indonesia)** — UI labels, error messages, and domain terms use Indonesian.
- Domain terms: "cuti" = leave, "karyawan" = employee, "pembinaan" = guidance, "peringatan" = warning, "pelatihan" = training.
- The company operates with a hierarchical approval chain for leave requests based on organizational structure.
